FAQ

Short answers to what players ask first. More in the README.

Getting the game running
What do I need to play?

Your own copy of Deadlock 2, installed from the CD, Steam or GOG, or just GOG's installer. OpenDeadlock brings the program; the game's pictures, sounds and maps come from your copy. No game data is included in the download.

Which version of Deadlock 2 works?

Any installed Deadlock 2, or GOG's installer itself. The port is made and checked against GOG's v1.20.

How do I install it?

Download the zip for your system from the downloads and unpack it anywhere: that folder is OpenDeadlock's own. Start opendeadlock; the first start asks for your Deadlock 2 folder or GOG's installer and copies the game's files into data/ beside the program (about 470 MB). Your Deadlock 2 is not changed.

macOS says the app is damaged or can't be opened

The macOS build is not notarized yet. Once, in OpenDeadlock's folder, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine . in Terminal, then start ./opendeadlock. It needs macOS 26 on Apple silicon.

How does it find the game on Linux?

Since 0.2.2 the first start asks, as on Windows and macOS: Choose folder and Choose installer open your desktop's own dialogs (through xdg-desktop-portal) or zenity's. Without either, its window says how to do it by hand: put GOG's installer (setup_deadlock_ii_shrine_wars_*.exe) in OpenDeadlock's folder, put gamedir=/path/to/Deadlock 2 (or the path of GOG's installer) in opendeadlock.ini beside the program, or put the files of OpenDeadlock's folder (opendeadlock and libSDL3.so.0) in the Deadlock 2 folder itself, where it plays in place, nothing copied. Any distribution with glibc 2.28 or later works, on x86_64 or arm64.

Is there an Android version?

Yes, made for tablets and played by touch, but so far it has only been tried on the emulator. Install the APK from the downloads (Android 7 or later) and start it: the first start asks for Deadlock 2 in Android's own pickers. Choose the Deadlock 2 folder you copied to the device, or GOG's installer (from Download, say), and the game is copied into the app's own folder, with its saves. Uninstalling the app deletes that folder, saves included.

Playing
Is it still the same game?

Yes. The rules and balance are the original's; only its bugs are fixed. The improvements change no rule, and most of them are settings you can turn off. The full changelog lists every difference.

Classic or modern interface?

The first start asks which one you want. The classic one is the original's screens, scaled; the modern one is drawn at your window's size, with panels over the live map. You can switch in Settings at any time.

Where are my saves?

In saves/ in OpenDeadlock's folder; campaigns' and network games' in saves/campaign/ and saves/multiplayer/. Custom scenarios and maps go in scenarios/.

Can I use my old saves from the original game?

The port keeps the original's save format, and the first start copies your saves over. Loading saves between the original and the port has not been fully tested yet, so keep a copy of any save you care about.

Where are the settings?

In the game's Settings page, and in opendeadlock.ini beside the program. The first start writes that file with every setting listed and explained in a comment. The keys, the original's and the new ones, are in KEYS.md.

Multiplayer
How do I play online?

Host a network game and you get a six-letter code; the others press F2 on Join a Game and type it. No port to open: games go through the public relay. Since 0.2.1 a hosted game is also listed publicly, in Join a Game and on the Games page; turn off "List publicly" beside Host a game to keep it to those who have the code.

Can I play with friends on other systems?

Yes: Windows and Mac players have played in one game. All players need the same version of OpenDeadlock. The port cannot play with the original game.

How does hotseat work?

Several players at one computer, taking turns. Start a multiplayer game and pick Hotseat as the connection, then how many play here. Each player has their own turn, event log and view, and a screen hides the last player's empire before the next sits down.

Problems
How do I report a bug?

Right after the problem, start the game once with --report (./opendeadlock --report, or opendeadlock.exe --report on Windows). It writes a report-….zip with the logs, your settings and your newest save, and quits. Attach it to an issue with what you did, or post it on Discord.

Is this official?

No. OpenDeadlock is a fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holder of Deadlock 2. It contains no game data: you need your own copy of the game.
